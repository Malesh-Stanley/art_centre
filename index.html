ABOUT US

A Place for Every Occasion

Notos Art Centre brings together entertainment, relaxation and memorable experiences in one place. Whether you are spending time with friends, celebrating an occasion or organising a corporate gathering, we provide a welcoming space for your experience.

From lively entertainment to private celebrations and professional events, Notos offers a venue designed for different moments and occasions.
